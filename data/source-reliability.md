# Source Reliability — learned, not declared

This file accumulates the output of `bin/source-retrospective.mjs`, the one quality
check that is not the writing model judging its own work. Method: re-run the same
queries for windows already published, reconstruct each window as it stood at
publication, and diff the answers against the figures archived in `data/facts/`.

**What drift is — and is not.** A published figure moving after publication is the
combined effect of late reporting, reclassification, and unfounding. This method
**cannot separate those**, and its findings must never be reported as "the source
changed its numbers." What it measures exactly is how much the figure we lead with
moves after the paper is out — which is what should govern how confidently that
figure is phrased, and which feeds the Score column in `sources-ranked.md` every
eighth edition or quarterly.

The first pass needs an edition at least 21 days old; early runs correctly report
that there is nothing to check yet.
